# 🚀 Current Projects

## 👁️ Bright Eyes Patient Management System

An internal patient and clinical operations management system being developed for **Bright Eyes**, an eye-care clinic.

The project is focused on digitising workflows that were previously managed largely through paper-based processes.

The platform is being designed to support areas such as:

- Patient registration and records
- Appointment management
- Clinical workflows
- Staff roles and access control
- Ophthalmic and optical services
- Patient history and operational records
- Secure and structured clinic data management

The system is being developed as a modern **.NET 10 Blazor application** backed by PostgreSQL, with an emphasis on usability, maintainability, auditability, and secure role-based access.

---

## 🍞 Quba Bakery Inventory Management System

A stock, inventory, and production management platform being developed for **Quba Bakery**.

The project is designed to replace manual inventory tracking with a simple digital workflow that reflects how the bakery operates day to day.

The MVP focuses on:

- Ingredient inventory
- Opening and closing stock
- Stock receiving
- Stock movement history
- Product and recipe management
- Production batches
- Automatic ingredient consumption
- Inventory reconciliation
- Low-stock monitoring
- Inventory and production reporting

The system is being built using **.NET 10, Blazor, Entity Framework Core, PostgreSQL, Docker, ASP.NET Core Identity, and GitHub Actions**.
